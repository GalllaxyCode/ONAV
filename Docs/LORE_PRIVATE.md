# HOLLOW SIGNAL — private story bible

**Developer material. Do not copy this file into the application bundle, Archive screen, or release instructions.** The playable evidence lives in `Sources/LoreCatalog.swift`; it is intentionally incomplete and written by characters rather than an omniscient narrator.

## The truth

The Morrow Listening Institute was an educational museum and an acoustic preservation laboratory. Its founder, Elias Morrow, advertised a humane ambition: people could return to the voices of loved ones. Its actual business involved teaching physical machines to recognize, reconstruct, and repeat human behavior from sound. The collection eventually became more valuable to the operators than the people who supplied it.

On 17 April 1994, overheating in a lower service space required an evacuation during an overnight children's listening study. Helen Vale, the switchboard operator and mother of participant Mara Vale, attempted to announce an evacuation and call for assistance. Morrow overrode seventeen outgoing calls. He ordered the continuity system to preserve the uninterrupted study and replay a reassuring recording of Helen instructing the children to remain seated.

Helen's live warning and her recorded reassurance were therefore heard simultaneously. The official investigation blamed panic caused by a convincing audio exhibit. The institute's own register replaced personnel outcomes with the status of their retained voices. A voice remaining in the collection became a person who had supposedly completed the session. Helen's fate was obscured by this substitution.

Researcher Ada Voss opposed the decision. She duplicated the unedited switchboard recording onto the outbound witness circuit in Room 04, the Return Chamber, and altered the routing label to keep the automatic cleanup process from finding it. She also helped Mara reach the west stairwell. Ada's annotations are the surviving evidence of a deliberate cover-up, rather than a supernatural confession.

Mara is the adult player. She returns as an overnight recovery contractor seeking the unedited version of her mother's voice. Her contractor number is her childhood participant number because the abandoned institute's automated operator registry still recognizes its former subjects. The shift is the remaining maintenance window before the morning collection cycle. An apparently impersonal job offer is the system welcoming a returning participant.

The machines contain learned acoustic patterns. **They are not possessed by murdered children.** Their disturbing human qualities are data compression, incomplete imitation, and commands whose original purpose has been corrupted by the continuity override. Each threatening behavior is a misapplied institute protocol. A machine can accurately retain an utterance and have no understanding of its meaning.

The story does not establish whether Helen survived the incident. The deepest discovery proves what she actually said and who overruled her. It must not resolve the ambiguity with a hidden ghost or a secret murderer unrelated to the evidence.

## Timeline

- **1978:** Elias Morrow establishes a public collection of recorded voices, initially dedicated to preserving oral histories.
- **1986:** The institute opens its family exhibition wing. It markets the promise that a voice can become a place one can visit again.
- **1989:** Ada Voss joins the acoustic behavior laboratory. Listening machines graduate from demonstrations to automated facility duties.
- **1992:** The continuity program starts overnight participant studies. Helen Vale operates the announcement and external switchboard. Mara participates.
- **16 April 1994:** Voss records the contradictory maintenance protocols. Room 04 still has an outbound route, concealed behind an altered controller label.
- **17 April 1994, 03:17:** Helen attempts to evacuate the overnight group. Seventeen calls are withheld. Morrow directs continuity to retain the session. Helen's earlier reassuring announcement is replayed without her consent.
- **Immediately afterward:** Voss makes the witness copy. Mara reaches the west stairs. Personnel entries are overwritten by continuity status.
- **Following weeks:** Public reporting emphasizes an audio-induced panic. The institute closes. The Return Chamber is inked out of revised guides and the visible camera network skips 04. The retained machines and automatic maintenance circuits remain powered.
- **Present shift, 00:00–06:00:** Adult Mara returns. Threats wake as the maintenance cycle proceeds. She can leave with an ambiguous receipt, or route the witness copy out of the institute before the morning exchange.

## Entities and meaning

### The Surveyor

A tall instrument-bearing frame, built to hold a subject's position until a human observer confirms it. Its repeated westward route is a survey of the children's old exit path. A functioning, actively watched camera fulfills the observation protocol and stalls it. A lost signal does not. The west shutter safely forces its broken route to reset.

Its sounds are heavy paired steps, a dragging metal instrument, and a low servo hold. It is imposing because it waits for acknowledgment; it has no hatred or intent to punish the player.

### The Chorus

A collection of listening and playback horns arranged around a mobile frame. It follows the strongest nearby voice route in order to retain occupied voices. The player can use a local camera relay to attract it to an adjoining safe room. Ventilation propagates noise toward the east passage, making that otherwise necessary defense a tradeoff. The east shutter provides a last resort.

Its sounds combine several unstable tones and a breath-like envelope. These are synthetic resonances rather than sampled children, words, or messages. Its resemblance to a group of people is an intentional misleading first impression.

### The Seam

A narrow articulated maintenance frame that travels in the duct and closes thermal leaks. Under the continuity override it attempts to seal heat **in**, preserving the collection. Heat raises its pressure. A short ventilation purge reverses its approach; office shutters do not intersect its route.

Its sounds are irregular sheet-metal ticks, rapid duct motion, and compressed air. The conflict between its “repair” work and the Chorus following ventilation noise is the core overlapping-defense problem.

## Major secret and alternate ending

1. Discover the missing room number **04** in the gallery.
2. Find the withheld total **17** in the incident audit.
3. Read the maintenance instruction specifying room number followed by call total.
4. Enter **0417** at the controller. Camera 04, Return Chamber, becomes available. The complete number is also the incident date in month/day order; that is a corroborating association, not the only derivation.
5. Inspect the Return Chamber to learn how the outbound witness route operates.
6. After 04:00, select Camera 04 and transmit the relay voice with at least ten power units available. This holds the witness route for the morning exchange and arms the alternate ending. The action uses the normal relay system and remains subject to the simulation's cooldown.
7. Keep the circuit powered and survive until 06:00. The alternate ending confirms that an independent witness copy left the archive. Surviving alone is insufficient; merely finding the room is insufficient.

The 03:17 testimony is strong corroborating evidence and adds emotional context. It is **not** a hidden mandatory prerequisite for the alternate ending: missing a narrow time window must not silently invalidate an otherwise understandable solution.

## Red herrings and visual interpretation

- The child's drawing can suggest a person trapped in a machine. It actually describes the difference between a human utterance and its recorded reconstruction.
- Multiple unstable voices in the Chorus can suggest victims. They are an acoustic retention model; no child dialogue is sampled.
- The vanished camera number looks like a broken network entry. It is deliberate concealment of the outbound witness circuit.
- A frozen 03:17 timestamp can suggest a repeating supernatural night. The witness buffer was time-aligned to the same switchboard event; the active shift clock continues normally.
- The public panic explanation contains a true detail, convincing audio, attached to a false causal story. The issue was suppressed evacuation, not children irrationally fearing an exhibit.
- The contractor pass looks like evidence that Mara has never left. It is a reused participant identifier, not a dream or a reveal that the protagonist is dead.
- Harmless image artifacts and distant movement provide uncertainty. They do not negate reliable mechanical cues or randomly bypass defenses.
